Methods & More

One question, many instruments.

My research is problem-focused, and problems rarely respect a single method. Depending on what a question needs, I listen through interviews and netnography, test through experiments and surveys, mine text at scale, or watch attention itself through eye-tracking and biometrics.

Listening In-depth interviews Netnography Interpretive analysis
Testing Lab & online experiments Scenario-based designs Survey research
Measuring at scale Text analysis & topic modeling Eye-tracking & biometrics Meta-analysis

Most projects braid at least two of these — interviews that seed experiments, forum text that grounds a scenario, biometrics beside self-reports.

iMotions Lab · UMass Amherst

Where attention becomes data.

At Isenberg’s iMotions biometric lab, I work with eye-tracking and physiological measurement to study what consumers feel — not only what they say. In the Consumer Robot Typology project, biometric data collected across three studies sits beside self-reports, letting present emotions be observed as well as reported.
